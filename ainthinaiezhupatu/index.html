ஐந்திணை எழுபது

Aintiṇai Eḻupatu

Seventy on the Five Landscapes · Fourteen poems per tiṇai

Seventy akam love poems across all five classical Tamil landscapes by Mōcikīraṉār, from the Patiṉeṇkīḻkaṇakku anthology. The companion piece to Aintiṇai Aimpatu — where that work gives ten poems per tiṇai, this gives fourteen, allowing deeper exploration of each landscape's full emotional arc: from first signal through crisis to resolution.

Seventy poems, five landscapes, one love. Each tiṇai section moves through its own complete sequence — signal, waiting, anxiety, reassurance, reunion or resolution — so that the collection as a whole traces not five separate stories but five versions of the same story, each told in the language of its own landscape.
