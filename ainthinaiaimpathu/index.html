ஐந்திணை ஐம்பது

Aintiṇai Aimpatu

Fifty on the Five Landscapes · Ten poems per tiṇai

Fifty akam love poems across all five classical Tamil landscapes by Māmulaṉār, from the Patiṉeṇkīḻkaṇakku anthology. Where Kār Nāṟpatu devotes forty poems to a single season, Aintiṇai Aimpatu surveys the complete akam grammar — ten poems for each of the five tiṇai — tracing the full arc of love from first union to patient waiting, quarrel, longing, and separation.

The five tiṇai together constitute a complete emotional map: not five separate kinds of love but five aspects of the same love, each given its own landscape, season, flower, bird, and feeling. Aintiṇai Aimpatu holds all five in one hand.
